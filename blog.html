The State of Single-Cell RNA Foundation Models: Beyond scGPT

The landscape of single-cell transcriptomics has shifted dramatically. While early models like scGPT and Geneformer proved that pre-training on large-scale cell atlases is viable, 2024–2025 papers have introduced a new generation of architectures focusing on cross-modality and context-specific refinement.

Recent updates emphasize the move toward multi-modal foundation models that don't just predict gene expression, but integrate chromatin accessibility (scATAC-seq) and spatial proteomics. Key papers like scFoundation v2 and the CellPLM series demonstrate that scaling laws in biology require different inductive biases compared to natural language processing.

In this post, we break down the performance benchmarks of the latest "Scaling Bio" papers and discuss why tokenization remains the biggest hurdle in genomic foundation models.
